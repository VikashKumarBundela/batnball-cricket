# Batnball 🏏

**Free cricket manager game. Play it on your phone, in your browser.**

▶️ **Play now:** https://batnball-cricket.netlify.app
💬 **Community:** https://t.me/+9wfWOfLEluNjMzk1

Pick a team, build your squad and chase the title. Start in the CPL, win it to join the ICL franchise league with a live player auction, then lead your nation at the World Cup.

## Features

- T20 match engine with toss, pitch, weather and dew
- Choose your XI, batting order, captain and vice-captain
- Make decisions ball by ball: next batter, next bowler, tactics
- CPL domestic league with playoffs
- ICL franchise league with mega and mini auctions, retention and live bidding
- Player development, hidden potential, scouting, contracts and budgets
- A living cricket world: news, awards, records, rivalries and Hall of Fame
- Daily challenge with streaks, achievements and a Road to Legend roadmap
- Light and dark themes, sound effects, works offline once loaded
- Your career saves on your device, with a backup code you can restore

## Free and Pro

The free version includes the full CPL and ICL career. International cricket, the Friends league and the Leaderboard are coming soon in **Batnball Pro**.

## Install on your phone

Open the game in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone), then choose **Add to Home screen**. It opens like an app.

## Run it yourself

Batnball is a single static page. Open `index.html` in a browser, or host the files on any static host such as Netlify or GitHub Pages.

Files:

- `index.html` is the whole game
- `manifest.webmanifest` and the icon images make it installable
- `og-image.png` is the link preview image
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` help search engines
